House rules

Respectful space for all students

Simple rules that keep StudyNest quiet, clean, and fair for everyone.

Quiet by design

Keep conversations low and use the group area for longer discussions. Silence your devices in the quiet zone.

No calls inside the quiet area

Take calls outside or in designated spots to avoid disrupting others who need full focus.

Food only at the snack corner

To keep desks clean and minimize distractions, enjoy food and drinks at the counter/snack corner.

15-minute grace period

If you're running a little late for your booking, a 15-minute grace period applies.

Cancel free before your slot

Need to reschedule? Cancel for free anytime before your booked slot starts.

Be considerate

Keep your space tidy, return borrowed items, and help maintain a positive environment for fellow students.
